Chandrasekaran Joseph Vijay, popularly known as Thalapathy Vijay, is an Indian politician, former actor, playback singer, and philanthropist, serving as the 9th Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu since May 2026. He founded and leads Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) after a three-decade career in Tamil cinema with 69 films, including blockbusters like Ghilli, Thuppakki, Kaththi, and Mersal.
